# Privacy Policy for Black Eagle Dog Management System

**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026

**Telthemweb Solutions Pvt Ltd** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Black Eagle Dog Management System mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

When you use our App, you may provide us with:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, company name, and password
- **Dog Information:** Dog names, breeds, photos, dates of birth, microchip numbers, health records, breeding records
- **Business Information:** Financial records, expense data, income records, bank account details (stored locally)
- **Handler Information:** Handler names, assignments, activity logs
- **Health Records:** Vaccination records, medical history, veterinary visit notes, treatment information
- **Photos:** Images of dogs uploaded through the app

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** App features used, interaction patterns, crash reports
- **Log Data:** IP address, access times, pages viewed

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

- **Service Provision:** Provide and maintain the dog management functionality
- **Account Management:** Create and manage your user account
- **Data Synchronization:** Sync your data across devices
- **Notifications:** Send WhatsApp notifications about dog activities (with your permission)
- **Analytics:** Improve app performance and user experience
- **Support:** Respond to your questions and provide customer support
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with applicable laws and regulations

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Data Storage

- Your data is stored on secure servers
- Photos and documents are stored using encrypted cloud storage (Cloudflare R2)
- User authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage

### 3.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- **Encryption:** Data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Authentication:** Secure user authentication with JWT tokens
- **Access Control:** Role-based access control to limit data access
- **Regular Updates:** Security patches and updates
- **Secure Storage:** Encrypted secure storage for sensitive data on device

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
